# Module 6: Create and Improve Work in Space

## What You Will Do

Save a reviewed result as a Page, find it again, edit it, and choose how to collaborate.

Recommended example: Create a Team Update Page and use the reviewed week 2 draft to update it.

You can use another workflow instead. Bring its corresponding result forward rather than starting a new task.

## Choose Your Surface

- **ChatGPT Work:** Ask to create or edit a Page and open it in Space. Keep tutoring in Work.
- **Codex:** Ask the same way from Codex using the available Page tools. Open the result in Space; keep tutoring in Codex.

Follow the activities in order, or ask to skip a topic you already know. Optional challenges never block progress.
