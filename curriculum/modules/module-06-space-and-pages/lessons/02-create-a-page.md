# Lesson 6.02: Create a Page and find it again

Turn your reviewed result into a document you can open, revise, and return to.

## Learn

A Page can hold a brief, plan, update, or handbook. Keep the important request near the top and supporting details where readers can find them.

Create it from content you already reviewed. Keep the reporting period and unresolved questions clear. Source links let you check the underlying material later.

Sharing a Page does not share your private chat. But content copied from a private source becomes visible to that Page’s readers. Check the destination before adding real work information.

## See It

“Create a Page from this reviewed update. Put the decision first, then progress and next steps. Keep source links in an evidence section.”

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Use your reviewed team-update draft to create a Team Update Page. If you chose another workflow, keep its corresponding reviewed result.

2. Ask the assistant in Work or Codex to create a Page in a new or appropriate space. Confirm the destination’s audience before adding content.

3. Open the actual Page and check its title, period, request, and source links.

4. Navigate away and find it again in Space. Save its link in your workflow notes.

**Success criteria:** You created and reopened a Page containing the reviewed result in the intended location.

## Challenge — Optional

Add a related child Page or another Page in the same space, then decide whether the grouping helps.

**Success criteria:** You can explain what belongs together without creating unnecessary structure.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring the Page link and its source content for the editing activity.
