# Lesson 6.04: Move a new draft into the reviewed Page

Now connect the repeating work to the place your reader will use.

## Learn

Your schedule prepares a draft. Your Page holds the reviewed result. A reliable handoff makes clear which version you are replacing.

Start by reviewing a new draft and asking for one specific Page revision. Keep the evidence links and unresolved questions. Check that the Page shows the intended reporting period afterward.

The schedule does not automatically gain access to a Page you created later. Keep this handoff manual first. If you change the scheduled action, verify its tools and test the new request.

## See It

“Use my reviewed week 2 draft to update the weekly section of this Page. Preserve the process notes and the separate evidence section.”

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Choose a second reviewed draft from your skill, manual test, or scheduled run.

2. Compare it with the current Page. Decide which sections should change and which should stay.

3. Ask the assistant to update those sections, then open the Page and check the result.

4. Add the Page link and your short review-and-update steps to the run card. Keep the task draft-only unless you deliberately authorize a different action.

**Success criteria:** You completed one real draft-to-Page handoff and can repeat it next time.

## Challenge — Optional

Design a task that prepares a proposed Page revision for review. Test its access without scheduling shared edits.

**Success criteria:** The proposal names the destination and review step without assuming automatic publication.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring the updated Page and manual handoff steps into the work-file activities.
