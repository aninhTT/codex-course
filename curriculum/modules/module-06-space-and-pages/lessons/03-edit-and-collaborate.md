# Lesson 6.03: Edit together and choose a sharing role

Practice asking for a precise change and making one yourself.

## Learn

A **focused edit** changes one part while preserving the rest. Name the section, passage, or request you want to improve.

You can edit a Page directly or ask the assistant to change it. Comments can help discuss a change with a collaborator. Available sharing roles depend on the content and account.

For Pages, view access is for readers, comment access is for feedback, and edit access is for coauthors. Access may also come from the parent Page or space. Check the saved audience before inviting someone.

## See It

“Shorten the background section to two sentences. Keep the decision and all next-step owners unchanged.”

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Read the Page as its audience. Make one small edit directly in the Page.

2. Ask the assistant for a different focused edit. Compare what changed and verify the facts stayed intact.

3. Inspect the available sharing roles and any inherited access.

4. Choose a reviewer. If you want to share, explicitly name the person and role and ask the tutor to apply that choice. Otherwise use a reader-role review here.

**Success criteria:** You made a direct edit, reviewed an assistant edit, and chose an audience and role deliberately.

## Challenge — Optional

Ask a reviewer what they would cut, or have the assistant role-play that reader. Resolve two useful comments.

**Success criteria:** The Page becomes clearer through specific feedback and you can explain your edits.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Keep the edited Page, reviewed changes, and chosen sharing role.
