# Lesson 6.01: Choose where the finished work belongs

Your draft is useful to you. Now decide how someone else should read and use it.

## Learn

In **ChatGPT Space**, you can find Pages and other saved work. A **space** groups related Pages around a topic or team. A **Page** is a document you and ChatGPT can edit together.

Your project helps you do the work with its chats and sources. A Page gives a reviewed result a place to live. A Site can provide an interactive hub later.

Start from the audience. Do they need a living brief, a meeting presentation, a few numbers, or just a short message? Put the output where they will use it. A team’s shared Google Doc can be the right destination too.

## See It

A project holds your research and drafting chats. A Page called “Team Update” holds the reviewed version readers return to.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Use the reviewed team-update draft from weekly-update-helper or its scheduled run. For another chosen workflow, use its corresponding result.

2. Name its audience and the action they need to take. Decide whether a Page would help them.

3. Open Space from your chosen desktop surface, or ask the tutor to help navigate to it while staying with the course.

4. Plan a simple Page and optional space for related updates. Keep it to the structure people will actually use.

**Success criteria:** You can explain the different jobs of the project, Page, and space using your own work.

## Challenge — Optional

Compare a Page with a short email or Google Doc. Choose the destination your reader would find easiest.

**Success criteria:** Your choice follows the audience’s needs rather than the number of features available.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Keep your audience, destination choice, and simple Page plan.
