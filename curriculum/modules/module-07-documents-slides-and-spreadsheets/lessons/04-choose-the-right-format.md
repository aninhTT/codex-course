# Lesson 7.04: Choose one output to keep using

You have more ways to make work. Choose the ones that earn your reader’s attention.

## Learn

The useful question is not how many formats you can create. It is which result your audience will use.

A Page can be a living record. A document can support a decision. Slides support a meeting. A spreadsheet explains numbers. A Site can provide a place to explore information.

Copies can become stale independently. A change in the document does not update the deck or Site unless you have built and verified that process. Keep the source and review date clear.

## See It

A team may need a short weekly Page and an occasional deck. It does not need three newly generated files every Friday.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Open the outputs you made or chose to explore in this module.

2. Ask the assistant to compare them against your actual audience and task.

3. Choose a recurring output and identify the formats you will use only occasionally. Record any formats you skipped rather than marking them completed.

4. Save the output links, source locations, and review dates in your workflow map. Bring the reviewed content to the Site activity.

**Success criteria:** You chose useful recurring outputs and know which copies would need manual updates.

## Challenge — Optional

Turn the same content into a second format you have not tried. Choose one winner and explain the tradeoff.

**Success criteria:** The format decision follows the audience’s need and includes a fact check of both versions.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring your chosen outputs and source links into the Site activity.
