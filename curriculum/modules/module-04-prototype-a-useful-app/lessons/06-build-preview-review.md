# Lesson 4.06: Build, Preview, Review

## Where You Are

You reviewed the plan. Now you can let Codex build the first version.

## Learn

The prototype build should follow the approved v1 plan. After building, preview it and review it. Do not judge only from Codex's summary. Open the local app or file and use it.

This is another version of the knowledge-work loop: connect the right context, let Codex build, review the artifact where it lives, then decide what should compound into a reusable workflow or checklist.

## Practice

Step 1: type this into Codex:

```text
Build the first version of this prototype based on the v1 plan we approved. Keep the scope tight. After building, tell me how to preview it and what I should review first.
```

Step 2: after previewing, type this:

```text
Review this prototype with me. Tell me what is working, what feels weak, and one useful iteration we could make later. Then summarize what we built, where it lives, and what I should carry into the next checkpoint.
```

**Success criteria:** The learner builds or starts the prototype, previews it, reviews it, and leaves with a clear summary of what to carry into the checkpoint.

## What You Made Or Decided

You built or started a prototype and reviewed it as a real artifact.
