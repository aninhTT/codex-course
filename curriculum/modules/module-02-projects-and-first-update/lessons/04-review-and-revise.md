# Lesson 2.04: Review it like someone who needs to act

You know things about your team that the assistant does not. Use that knowledge to improve the draft.

## Learn

**Review** means checking the actual result against its intended job. Clear writing is useful, but you also need accurate facts, a visible request, and the right level of detail.

Ask the assistant for a critical review, then make your own judgment. It can miss the same assumption when reviewing its own work. You bring the business context.

Use the updated AI Expectations as the standard for work you share. You should understand the result and be able to stand behind it.

## See It

“Read this as a team lead with two minutes. What is unclear? What would you cut? Which claim needs a source check?”

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Read your draft as its intended audience. Find the action or decision they need to take.

2. Ask the assistant for three specific weaknesses. Compare them with what you noticed.

3. Choose one improvement and make it. Ask for a focused edit or change the text yourself.

4. Compare the revision with the original. Check that the edit did not alter a source fact.

**Success criteria:** You improved one real weakness and checked the revision against the source.

## Challenge — Optional

Ask for a skeptical reader’s review. Resolve two useful comments and explain why you declined another.

**Success criteria:** You can defend your editorial choices rather than accepting every suggestion.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Keep your reviewed draft and the reason for the change you made.
