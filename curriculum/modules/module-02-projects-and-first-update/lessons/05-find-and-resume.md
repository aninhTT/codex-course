# Lesson 2.05: Find your work and try a task on your own

Prove you can find the result again, then try a small task without a full template.

## Learn

A clear chat title helps you find an outcome. A saved source link helps you verify it. A **handoff note** gives a new chat the current state of the work.

Keep learning here in Work or Codex. You only need a handoff if you choose to start elsewhere. That note should name the purpose, sources, output location, decisions, and next step.

Learning to delegate includes writing your own request. Use the task ingredients from Lesson 2.03, then judge the result. The tutor can offer hints without taking over.

## See It

A useful chat title is “Friday update: draft and review.” A useful handoff names the saved draft and the source notes, not just “continue our work.”

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Rename the chat for the outcome and reopen your saved draft from its project or output location.

2. For the team-update path, write your own request to summarize the decisions and blockers in week 1. If you chose another workflow, practice a step within that same task.

3. Write and run the request yourself. Include the outcome, sources, time period when relevant, and format.

4. Tell the tutor what worked and what you would change. Save a short recap and your Clean Draft candidate.

**Success criteria:** You found your output again and completed a small self-directed task.

## Challenge — Optional

Resume the work in a new chat using only your handoff and sources. Identify anything the recap left out.

**Success criteria:** The new chat can continue without inventing missing history.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring a draft you want to improve with Clean Draft and your workflow brief.
