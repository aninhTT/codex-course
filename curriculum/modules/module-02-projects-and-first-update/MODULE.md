# Module 2: Give Your Workflow a Home

## What You Will Do

Organize sources, write a short work briefing, and produce a useful draft.

Recommended example: Keep the week 1 notes and template in a Team Updates project. Save the original update for Clean Draft.

You can use another workflow instead. Bring its corresponding result forward rather than starting a new task.

## Choose Your Surface

- **ChatGPT Work:** Use a ChatGPT project for related chats, instructions, and uploaded or connected sources. Use a local folder project when the work needs it.
- **Codex:** Use the course project or a separate local project. Ask for help inspecting the folder and saving the result.

Follow the activities in order, or ask to skip a topic you already know. Optional challenges never block progress.

Your project, workflow brief, original draft, and one independently completed task.
