# Lesson 8.05: Finish the foundation with a workflow you can reuse

Find what you made, show how it fits together, and choose what to keep doing.

## Learn

Your foundation can work in either Work or Codex. A project holds context, skills save methods, a recorded step captures a routine, and a schedule prepares recurring work.

You decide which reviewed results belong in a Page, work file, or Site. Keep the links and ownership clear so the workflow remains useful after the course.

You can finish here and take the closing lesson. “Build further with Codex” offers local prototype and GitHub practice in Module 9. Module 10 covers longer work and Goals in supported Work or Codex setups.

## See It

A useful map lists the current source, tested skill, saved schedule, review owner, and real output links. It marks manual handoffs as manual.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Use workflow-map-template.md to locate your sources, skills, capture step, schedule, review, Page, files, and Site.

2. Open the key outputs and check the status of each. Keep any skipped activity or workaround clearly labeled.

3. Choose whether to keep, pause, or delete your practice schedule. Record who reviews any task you keep.

4. Ask for a short completion recap and choose closing, Module 9, or Module 10.

**Success criteria:** You can locate your work, explain its handoffs, and name the next useful step.

## Challenge — Optional

Use the workflow on a new real input without the lesson prompts. Improve one instruction from the experience.

**Success criteria:** You can repeat the workflow and explain the one change that made it more useful.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Save your workflow map and current progress. Choose closing or an optional extension.
