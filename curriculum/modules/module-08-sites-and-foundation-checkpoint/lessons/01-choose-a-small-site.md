# Lesson 8.01: Choose a team hub worth building

Give your team one useful place to find information or complete a small task.

## Learn

A **Site** is a hosted website or small app you can build with ChatGPT or Codex. It is useful when navigation or an interaction helps people use the information.

Choose one job: find the latest update, filter next steps by owner, or navigate onboarding resources. A Page may be enough when people mostly need to read and edit prose.

Start from a prompt and reviewed content. You do not need a local code project first. If you want deeper technical control, the Codex building extension is available after the foundation.

## See It

“Build a team-update website with this week’s progress, decisions, and next steps. Let readers filter the sample action list by owner.”

## Optional Demo

[Codex Sites](https://www.loom.com/share/1cee2c493d364e9fa291de56bab91374). Watch if seeing the steps helps; the tutor can teach the activity without the video.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Plan a Team Update Hub using the reviewed update and Page from earlier modules. If you chose another workflow, make the Site serve that same task.

2. Name the audience and one job the Site should make easier. Compare it briefly with the Page you already have.

3. Choose one page and at most two useful interactions for the first version.

4. Give the assistant your reviewed sources and ask for a small plan. Remove features that do not help the first test.

**Success criteria:** Your plan names a user, purpose, reviewed content, and a small first version.

## Challenge — Optional

Ask a potential reader what they would look for first, or role-play that reader. Adjust the plan from the answer.

**Success criteria:** One design choice changes because of the user’s actual need.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring your small Site plan and reviewed content to the build.
