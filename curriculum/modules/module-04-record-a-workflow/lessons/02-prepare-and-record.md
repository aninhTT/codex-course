# Lesson 4.02: Record the task and inspect the skill

Make the first recording short enough that you can explain every step it captures.

## Learn

A clean demonstration has a start, a few steps, and a clear finish. Close unrelated windows and use sample information. Stop recording as soon as the task is complete.

In the desktop app, use Work or Codex. Open Plugins, then the + menu, then **Record a skill** where available. Review the recording request and demonstrate the task when you are ready.

After recording, inspect the skill draft. It should explain when to use the workflow, what inputs change, and how to check the result. A recording of one week should not permanently lock the skill to that week.

## See It

If your recording selects “October 19–23,” the reusable instruction should ask for a reporting period rather than always selecting those dates.

## Optional Demo

[How to record a skill](https://www.loom.com/share/1b8d4305c7514781ae863a8026441953). Watch if seeing the steps helps; the tutor can teach the activity without the video.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Open week-01-notes.md and prepare to save a copy as inputs/team-update/current-notes.md in your course practice area. Use the equivalent input for another chosen workflow.

2. Use Record a skill in Work or Codex. Let the tutor help find the control, then complete the recording consent step yourself.

3. Demonstrate the task and stop recording at the agreed finish.

4. Ask the tutor to show the generated skill’s steps. Find one captured preference and one value that should change next time.

**Success criteria:** You stopped the recording and inspected the resulting skill draft, including its changing inputs.

## Challenge — Optional

Make the draft clearer by explaining a hidden preference the recording could not reveal, such as why you choose a file name.

**Success criteria:** The skill now states that preference explicitly.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring the captured skill draft and the fixed details you need to make reusable.
