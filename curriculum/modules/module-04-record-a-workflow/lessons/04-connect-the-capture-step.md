# Lesson 4.04: Connect the captured step to your workflow

Give each skill a clear job so you can reuse the pieces independently.

## Learn

Your capture skill might gather a report. Your update skill turns information into a draft. Clean Draft checks the writing. These are useful layers when each one has a clear input and output.

Try the sequence manually before scheduling it. Check that the next step reads the new input rather than an old draft with a similar name.

Some capture steps depend on your desktop. Keep that dependency visible for Module 5. You can still schedule draft preparation while gathering an input yourself.

## See It

Capture the current report → draft the update → review its wording → check the result. Each arrow is a handoff to test.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Run or demonstrate your capture step and identify its saved output.

2. Give the current notes copy to weekly-update-helper and prepare the team-update draft. If you chose another workflow, use its matching skill.

3. Use Clean Draft where writing quality matters, then check one fact against the input.

4. Ask the tutor to save a short handoff listing each skill’s job, files, and required tools. Carry it into scheduling.

**Success criteria:** You tested the manual sequence and identified which parts need your computer or your own preparation.

## Challenge — Optional

Remove a step that adds no value, then rerun the sequence. Explain why the simpler version is enough.

**Success criteria:** The remaining steps still produce the required result with less work.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring the manual sequence, each skill’s job, and its required tools to scheduling.
