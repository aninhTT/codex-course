# Module 4: Record a Workflow and Reuse It

## What You Will Do

Demonstrate a small computer task, inspect its skill, and replay it with a new input.

Recommended example: Record preparing a current notes file, then give that input to weekly-update-helper.

You can use another workflow instead. Bring its corresponding result forward rather than starting a new task.

## Choose Your Surface

- **ChatGPT Work:** Use the desktop app’s Record a skill flow. Keep the course tutor here while you demonstrate.
- **Codex:** Use the same Record a skill flow in the desktop app. The resulting skill can support your Codex workflow.

Follow the activities in order, or ask to skip a topic you already know. Optional challenges never block progress.
