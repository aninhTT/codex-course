# Lesson 10.01: Choose a normal task or a longer Goal

Use a persistent objective when the work needs a clear finish line across many steps.

## Learn

An ordinary request can already delegate a multi-step task. **Goal mode** adds a persistent objective for longer work where the next step depends on what the assistant discovers.

Use a Goal for a substantial audit or evidence-based research task you can verify. Your weekly-update skill and schedule are usually enough for routine updates.

Goals are not exclusively a Codex topic. Use supported Goal controls in Work or Codex on desktop. For hosted web work, use Work with an explicit outcome, boundaries, and review criteria.

## See It

“Audit the sources and outputs in my workflow and list every stale copy with evidence” has a finish line. “Keep making my workflow better” does not.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Use an audit of your team-update workflow’s sources and copies, or a longer task within your saved workflow choice.

2. Compare a normal request, a skill, a schedule, and a Goal for that task.

3. Name the output you would expect and how you could verify completion.

4. Decide whether to draft a Goal. Keep it unactivated while learning to write one.

**Success criteria:** You chose a suitable longer task or explained why a normal request is enough.

## Challenge — Optional

Write an example that sounds substantial but has no checkable finish line. Explain what would need to change.

**Success criteria:** You can distinguish effort from evidence of completion.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring your chosen longer task and the output you expect to verify.
