# Module 5: Put a Useful Skill on a Schedule

## What You Will Do

Test your recurring request, schedule it weekly or monthly, and inspect the saved task.

Recommended example: Schedule weekly-update-helper to prepare a Friday team-update draft for review.

You can use another workflow instead. Bring its corresponding result forward rather than starting a new task.

## Choose Your Surface

- **ChatGPT Work:** Ask the tutor to create the tested task and open Scheduled to verify it.
- **Codex:** Ask from Codex in the desktop app, then verify the saved task in Scheduled. You do not need to move the course to Work.

Follow the activities in order, or ask to skip a topic you already know. Optional challenges never block progress.
