# Lesson 5.01: Choose what to repeat and when

Take the skill you just tested and give it a useful weekly or monthly rhythm.

## Learn

A **scheduled task** runs a saved request at a chosen time. The skill defines how to do the work; the schedule defines when to start.

Choose a time that supports a real habit. A Friday update should be ready before its reader needs it. A monthly report should run after the source data is available.

For the first task, prepare a draft for your review. Returning to the same chat keeps its context; a standalone task can give each run a separate result. Choose the pattern that makes the work easiest to review.

## See It

Weekly: prepare Friday’s update from current notes. Monthly: prepare a summary on the first weekday after the reporting period closes.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Use the tested weekly-update-helper, or the skill for your saved workflow choice. Say when its reader needs the result.

2. Pick weekly or monthly timing. Name the time and timezone; do not assume everyone means the same local time.

3. Choose whether runs should return to this chat or start fresh.

4. Ask the tutor to summarize what will happen and where you will review the draft.

**Success criteria:** You chose a meaningful cadence and can explain the method, timing, and review destination.

## Challenge — Optional

Compare a weekly draft with a monthly summary. Describe which inputs and review questions would change.

**Success criteria:** Your monthly version has a clear reporting period rather than simply a longer weekly prompt.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Keep your chosen cadence, timezone, run pattern, and review destination.
