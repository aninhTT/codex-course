# Lesson 1.01: Choose Work or Codex and try your first task

You can take this whole course in ChatGPT Work or Codex. Pick one, then put it to work.

## Learn

An **agent** is AI that can use tools and take steps toward a result. You describe the outcome, give it useful context, and review what it makes.

For most employees who are not engineers, **ChatGPT Work is the recommended starting point**. It can gather information, work across connected tools, and create an update, analysis, document, or other useful result.

**Codex supports the foundation activities too.** Choose it if you prefer its technical detail. We especially recommend it for technical work, such as inspecting a GitHub repository or developing an app. Work can also build useful tools; these are recommendations, not exclusive capabilities.

Use ordinary **Chat** when you want a quick answer or conversation. Use Work or Codex when you want the assistant to carry out a task. You can stay in your chosen surface for the lessons and tutoring.

Later, “Build further with Codex” adds local app files, previews, and GitHub practice. You do not need that extension to create useful work in the foundation.

## See It

“What belongs in a weekly update?” asks for advice.

“Use these notes to draft my weekly update for team leads. Put the decision they need to make first.” delegates a result.

## Practice

Your tutor guides one step at a time in Work or Codex. Continue the recommended team-update example, or adapt the steps to your saved workflow choice.

1. Choose Work or Codex. Tell your tutor your choice so it gives you the right instructions.

2. Start with the recommended weekly team update using the course notes. If you already have another task in mind, tell the tutor and use that instead.

3. Give the assistant a few notes and ask for a short draft for a named audience. If using the sample, ask it to read week-01-notes.md from the course pack.

4. Read the result. Point to one useful detail and one thing you would change.

**Success criteria:** You chose a surface, produced a first short draft, and can explain why an agent helped with this task.

## Challenge — Optional

Try the same small request in the other surface. Compare what helps you work; switching is optional.

**Success criteria:** You can explain your preferred surface using the actual experience.

## Quick Check

What did the assistant assume? Which fact or behavior did you verify yourself?

## Carry Forward

Bring your chosen surface, audience, first draft, and one requested improvement.
