# Weekly Team Update — [reporting period]

## Request or Decision

State the action or decision the reader needs to consider. Flag missing decision owners or dates separately.

## Progress

Use two or three source-backed points.

## Blockers or Open Questions

Say what is missing and why it matters. Do not guess an answer.

## Next Steps

Use the owner, action, and date when the source provides them.

## Questions for the Author

List gaps that need a person to resolve them. Keep these separate from the ready-to-paste update.

## Evidence for Review

Keep source locations here so the author can verify the draft. This section can remain outside a short message sent to teammates.
